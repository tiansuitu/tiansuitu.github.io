An informal, dated record of questions and working notes while reading Rick Durrett's Probability: Theory and Examples (5th edition), as part of my preparation for STAT5005, the qualifying examination for the PhD programme in Statistics at CUHK.

Unresolved questions

  1. Exercise 2.4.2 (p. 69) — Mutual independence of normalized increments

    November 1, 2026

    Let \(Y_n=X_n/|X_{n-1}|\), where \(X_0=(1,0)\). Prove that \(\sigma(Y_1,\ldots,Y_n)\subseteq\sigma(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\), and use the independence of \(Y_{n+1}\) from \((X_1,\ldots,X_n)\) to establish mutual, rather than merely pairwise, independence of \((Y_n)_{n\ge1}\).

  2. Example 2.4.10 (p. 69) — Shannon's asymptotic equipartition property

    November 1, 2026

    Let \(\pi_n=\prod_{i=1}^n p(X_i)\) and \(H=-\sum_{k=1}^r p(k)\log p(k)\). Starting from \(-n^{-1}\log\pi_n\to H\) almost surely, prove that for every \(\varepsilon>0\),

    \[ \mathbb P\!\left(e^{-n(H+\varepsilon)}\leq\pi_n \leq e^{-n(H-\varepsilon)}\right)\longrightarrow 1. \]
